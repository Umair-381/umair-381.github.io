Research Focus

Hydrologist working on flood risk in large river basins and rapidly urbanising regions, combining satellite remote sensing, ensemble machine learning and extreme value statistics. Current work spans the Lancang–Mekong system, quantifying flood-driven losses to rice production under CMIP6 scenarios and testing the stationarity assumptions underlying operational flood-frequency thresholds.
